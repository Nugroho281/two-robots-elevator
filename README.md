One line of funny awkward small talk that one robot says to another while they ride an elevator together — for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty "Two Robots in an Elevator".

## The line

> I keep nodding at the robot in the mirror. He never nods back.

62 characters, one line. The angle: a robot politely greets its own reflection in the elevator mirror — its self-recognition fails, so the greeting is never returned. Machine logic (failed self-recognition) behind forced politeness, awkwardness that lands on the first read.

Chosen by blind self-judge over two other variants (weight-sensor accusation, elevator-voice jealousy): 4-0, margin 2 in every game.
